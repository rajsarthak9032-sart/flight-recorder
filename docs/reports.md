# Research Reports & Metrology Publishing

## Mission

Research Reports transform raw experiment datasets and diagnostic investigations into self-contained, ISO/IEC 42001-compliant technical reports.

---

## Report Sections

1. **Header & Metadata**: Unique Report ID, ISO/IEC 42001 audit profile, timestamp.
2. **Real Runtime Boundary Notice**: Explicit disclosure whenever hardware execution is unverified.
3. **Executive Summary**: High-level synthesis of trials, latency, and fault findings.
4. **Research Objective**: Stated workload goal and prompt specification.
5. **Methodology & Environment**: Target model, provider, hardware, OS, and sampling rules.
6. **Provenance Distribution**: Quantitative breakdown of `REAL`, `MOCK`, `BLOCKED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `LEGACY`.
7. **Statistical Summary**: Bessel sample standard deviation, median, mean, min, max.
8. **Trial Matrix**: Complete record of all individual trials.
9. **Failure Diagnostics Findings**: Grounded root cause findings with observable traces.
10. **Replay Lineage Tree**: Parent-child lineage diagram.
11. **Pairwise Empirical Comparisons**: Factual metric deltas without arbitrary winner badges.
12. **Limitations & Boundary Conditions**: Rigorous statement of constraints and scope.
13. **Factual Conclusion**: Final data-driven summary.
